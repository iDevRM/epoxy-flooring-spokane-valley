Garage Floor Epoxy

The most common project we do in Spokane Valley. A coated garage floor stands up to hot tire pickup, oil, and the salt and slush tracked in every winter, instead of soaking it into bare concrete.

If your garage floor has stains, chalking, or hairline cracks from freeze-thaw cycles, this is the fix. Ignored, those cracks widen every winter and the concrete surface keeps breaking down.

Basement Floor Epoxy

For basements being converted into a gym, workshop, or rec room, an epoxy floor blocks moisture coming up through the slab and gives you a surface that is easy to keep clean below grade.

Left uncoated, a below-grade concrete floor holds humidity and can develop a musty smell over time, especially in older Spokane Valley homes with less modern vapor barriers.
