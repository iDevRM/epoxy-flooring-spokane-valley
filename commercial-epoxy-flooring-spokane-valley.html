Commercial Epoxy Flooring in Spokane Valley, WA

Commercial epoxy flooring gives shops, warehouses, and light-industrial spaces in Spokane Valley a durable, easy-to-clean surface that stands up to daily foot and equipment traffic without dusting, staining, or wearing through in high-use lanes.

What Does Commercial Epoxy Flooring Involve?

The process follows the same core steps as a residential job — diamond grinding, crack repair, primer, base coat, and topcoat — but we scale material selection and slip-resistant texture to the traffic level and use of the space, whether that is a retail showroom, an auto shop bay, or a warehouse aisle.

When Do You Need Commercial Epoxy Flooring?

If your current concrete floor is dusting constantly, staining from oil or chemicals, or becoming difficult to keep clean between inspections, it is time to coat it. New tenants build out are also a common time to install, before shelving and equipment go in.

Why Does Commercial Floor Wear Happen So Fast?

Bare concrete in a commercial setting takes constant abrasion from foot traffic, carts, and equipment, plus chemical exposure from cleaning products, oils, or manufacturing byproducts depending on the business. Without a sealed surface, that combination wears through the top layer of concrete within a few years, leaving dust and a rough, hard-to-clean texture.

What Affects the Cost of Commercial Epoxy Flooring in Spokane Valley?

Commercial pricing starts in the same $4 to $10 per square foot range as residential work but can run higher for large open floor plans needing heavy-duty traffic coatings, or lower per square foot on large-area jobs due to efficiency of scale. Working around active equipment, drains, or phased scheduling to limit downtime also affects the final price.

Epoxy vs. Polished Concrete: Which Do You Need?

Polished concrete is more durable against heavy abrasion long term but offers no chemical resistance and shows stains more easily. Epoxy coating is the better choice when chemical spills, oil, or stains are a regular concern, which covers most shops, auto bays, and light-industrial floors in the Spokane Valley area.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can commercial epoxy flooring be installed without shutting down my business for long?

We can section off and coat areas in phases so part of your shop or warehouse stays operational, and we often schedule commercial jobs for evenings or weekends to minimize downtime.

Is commercial epoxy flooring slip resistant enough for OSHA compliance?

Yes, we add a slip-resistant aggregate to the topcoat on commercial floors, and can adjust the texture level based on the specific traffic and wet/dry conditions in your space.

How does commercial epoxy handle forklift and heavy equipment traffic?

A solid epoxy or polyaspartic system rated for heavy traffic resists rutting and abrasion from forklifts and rolling equipment far better than bare or painted concrete, especially in consistent traffic lanes.
